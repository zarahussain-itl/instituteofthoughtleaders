Why now

Organisations need people who can support wellbeing at work.

Since 1 December 2025, Victorian employers have been required to identify and control psychosocial hazards, such as high job demands, in the same way they manage physical risks. Similar obligations apply across Australia.

That is driving demand for coaches who understand workplaces, and for employers to build that capability in-house.
