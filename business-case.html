The numbers

The three calculations your approver will ask for.

The template sets each one out so you only add your own figures. We supply exact fees and dates in a written proposal.

Total cost

Program fees, less group or founding discounts, plus participant time and any backfill or travel.

Cost per certified coach

Total cost divided by the number of participants who certify.

External coaching comparison

People coached × sessions each × cost per session, against the cost of certifying your own coaches.
