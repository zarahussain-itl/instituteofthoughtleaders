Privacy policy

Last updated [DATE]

This policy explains how Zara Hussain, trading as the Institute of Thought Leaders (ITL, ABN 49 122 630 940), collects, uses, stores and shares personal information. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

1. What we collect

  • Contact details: name, email, phone, organisation and role, when you enquire, register or subscribe.
  • Enrolment details: work experience, eligibility information and, for sponsored places, your employer's details.
  • Learning and assessment records: attendance, assignments, assessor feedback, practicum logs and results.
  • Coaching recordings: recordings of practice and assessed coaching sessions, which you submit for assessment.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment provider. We don't store full card numbers.
  • Website data: pages visited and device information, collected through cookies and analytics.

2. Sensitive information

Coaching conversations can touch on health, wellbeing or other sensitive matters. We only collect sensitive information with consent, and only when it is needed for learning or assessment. Practice clients who appear in recordings must give written consent before a session is recorded. Students are taught to avoid recording identifying details wherever possible.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and deliver program previews
  • To enrol you, deliver the program, assess your work and issue your credential
  • To process payments and invoices
  • To send newsletters and program updates, only if you have opted in
  • To improve our programs and website

4. What we share with employers

If your employer sponsors your place, we share only attendance, assessment progress and completion status. We never share the content of coaching sessions, recordings or assessor feedback with your employer without your written consent.

5. Other disclosures

6. Storage, security and retention

We store information in secure systems with access limited to staff and assessors who need it. Coaching recordings are deleted [N] months after your final assessment result, or sooner if you ask once assessment is complete.

We keep enrolment and results records for [N] years so we can confirm your credential. We delete or de-identify other information when we no longer need it.

7. Access and correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. We'll respond within 30 days. Contact our privacy officer using the details below.

8. Marketing and cookies

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some website features may not work without them.

9. Complaints

If you're concerned about how we've handled your information, contact our privacy officer first. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au. See also our complaints process.
