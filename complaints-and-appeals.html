Policies

Complaints and appeals

If something hasn't met your expectations, we want to know and put it right.

Our commitments

  • Making a complaint is free.
  • We treat every complaint confidentially and fairly.
  • You won't be disadvantaged in your studies for raising a concern.
  • You can bring a support person to any meeting.

How to make a complaint

  1. 1Talk to us. Many concerns can be resolved quickly with your facilitator or mentor coach.
  2. 2Put it in writing. If that doesn't resolve it, email info@instituteofthoughtleaders.com with "Complaint" in the subject line. Tell us what happened and what outcome you're looking for.
  3. 3We acknowledge it within [2] business days and name the person handling it.
  4. 4We respond in writing within [20] business days with our findings and what we will do. If it will take longer, we'll tell you why.

Appealing an assessment result

If you disagree with an assessment decision, you can appeal in writing within [14] days of receiving your result. A different assessor, who was not involved in the original decision, will review your submission against the ITL Coaching Competency Standard. We'll give you the outcome within [15] business days.

Concerns about conduct

You can report a concern about a faculty member, student or graduate under the ITL Code of Ethics using the same process. If someone is at immediate risk, call 000 first.

If you're still not satisfied

You can ask for your complaint to be reviewed by [INDEPENDENT REVIEWER]. You can also contact:

  • Consumer Affairs Victoria for consumer issues, at consumer.vic.gov.au
  • The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner for privacy issues, at oaic.gov.au
