Employer questions

Frequently asked questions

Can a private cohort be tailored to our sector?

Yes. The core curriculum and assessment stay the same so the credential means the same thing, but case examples, practice scenarios and practicum projects are built around your sector and workplace.

Will coaching conversations stay confidential?

Yes. We report to you on attendance and assessment progress, never on what participants discuss. Internal coaches also learn how to agree confidentiality with the people they coach and their managers.

Is this suitable for shift and rostered workers?

Yes. Live sessions are recorded, online learning is self-paced, and private cohorts can schedule the face-to-face intensive around your rosters.

What if a participant leaves our organisation mid-program?

You can substitute another eligible participant in the early weeks of the program at no cost, or transfer them to the next intake once. See our policies.

What if a participant doesn't pass assessment?

They can resit once with the next intake at no extra cost.

Can we see the teaching before we commit?

Yes. Join a complimentary program preview, or ask us to run a private preview for your HR or leadership team.

Is this a nationally recognised qualification?

No. ITL certifications are professional credentials aligned with recognised bodies (ABNLP, the ICF Core Competencies, ABH and Clinical Hypnosis Australia), not AQF qualifications. Coaching is not a regulated profession in Australia.

Will our internal coaches replace our EAP?

No. Coaching complements your EAP and clinical services. Graduates learn to recognise when someone needs more support and how to refer them safely.

Do you deliver outside Melbourne?

Online components are available anywhere in Australia. Private cohorts can hold the intensive at your site. Contact us for intensive locations.

How will we know it made a difference?

Every participant completes a workplace case study with a measured outcome, so you see coaching applied to real challenges in your organisation before they graduate.
