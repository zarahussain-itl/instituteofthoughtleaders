Is this you?

You already develop people. Now make it your profession.

You have years inside organisations. You're the person colleagues come to when they're stuck, and you've seen what unclear goals, unhelpful thinking and overload do to performance. What you don't yet have is a structured method and a credential that lets you do this work formally, inside your organisation or as an independent coach.

This is ITL's foundation coaching certification. It gives you a practical, evidence-informed coaching method you can use from your first week, and a credential corporate clients understand.

Outcomes

When you graduate, you'll be able to

Run a complete coaching engagement

From contracting and goal-setting through to review, with a clear structure for every session.

Shift unhelpful thinking

Use cognitive-behavioural techniques to help people challenge the beliefs holding back their performance.

Build habits that last

Apply research on attention, motivation and habit formation so change sticks after coaching ends.

Hold high-stakes conversations

Build rapport quickly, listen for what isn't said, and ask questions that move people forward.

Measure and report results

Set baselines, track progress and report outcomes to the managers and sponsors who fund coaching.

Practise ethically, within scope

Know where coaching ends and when to refer someone to EAP, a GP or mental health support.

Curriculum

Six modules, built for real workplaces.

Each module combines short online lessons, a live practice session and a workplace application task.

  1. 01

    Foundations of corporate coaching

    What coaching is and isn't, contracting with individuals and sponsors, confidentiality, and the coaching relationship.

  2. 02

    Listening, questioning and rapport

    Active listening, powerful questions and the language patterns that build trust in workplace conversations.

  3. 03

    Goals and solution-focused coaching

    Setting goals that matter to the person and the organisation, and coaching towards solutions rather than problems.

  4. 04

    Cognitive-behavioural coaching

    Identifying and reframing unhelpful thinking, and linking thoughts, emotions and behaviour at work.

  5. 05

    The neuroscience of performance

    How attention, stress, motivation and habit work in the brain, and what that means for focus and digital load.

  6. 06

    Ethics, measurement and your practice

    Scope of practice and referral, measuring and reporting outcomes, and positioning your coaching inside organisations.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

I've never coached formally. Can I still enrol?

Yes. This is ITL's foundation program and teaches coaching from the ground up. What we require is meaningful corporate experience, typically five years or more, because that's what makes our graduates credible inside organisations.

I work full time. How much time will this take?

Plan for about [HOURS] hours a week, including online lessons, a live session and practice. Live sessions run outside core business hours and are recorded, and the program avoids peak business periods like end of financial year.

What if I miss a live session?

Every live session is recorded. You can catch up on the recording and make up practice with your peer group. [MAKE-UP POLICY FOR THE INTENSIVE]

Is this certification recognised?

The program is aligned with ABNLP standards and leads to the ITL Certified Corporate Performance Coach credential. Coaching is not a regulated profession in Australia, and this is a professional certification rather than a nationally recognised (AQF) qualification. What corporate clients look for is evidence you can coach well and report results, which is exactly what the assessment requires.

How is this different from an online life coaching course?

Three ways. Every student has corporate experience, so you learn alongside peers who understand organisations. The methods are evidence-based and built for workplace goals. And you're assessed on observed coaching and real practicum hours, not a multiple-choice quiz.

Is the approach actually evidence-based?

The core methods, cognitive-behavioural coaching, solution-focused coaching and goal-setting, have a published research base in workplace settings. In line with our value of integrity, we teach what the evidence supports and are clear about its limits.

Can my employer pay for it?

Yes. We invoice organisations directly and can provide a summary of learning outcomes and workplace benefits to support your professional development request.

Will I get clients or a coaching role afterwards?

We won't promise income, because no honest program can. We do teach you how to position coaching inside organisations and report outcomes in business terms, which is what wins internal roles and contracts. Graduates can also apply to join ITL's practitioner network as it grows.

Is coaching the same as counselling or therapy?

No. Coaching supports people with goals and performance. It doesn't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. You'll learn to recognise when someone needs clinical support and how to refer them safely.

Which stream should I start with?

Most people start here. Corporate Performance Coach gives you the core coaching method. Choose Executive and Team Coach if you already coach and want to work with senior leaders and teams, or Workplace Wellbeing Coach if your focus is stress and resilience programs.
