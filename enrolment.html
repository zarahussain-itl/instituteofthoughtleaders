What's included

Everything is included.

  • All online lessons and resources
  • Live online sessions and recordings
  • The face-to-face intensive
  • A dedicated 1:1 mentor coach, the same person throughout, plus practicum supervision
  • Assessment, certificate and digital badge
  • 12 months in the ITL alumni community

Ways to pay

Choose what suits you.

  • Pay in full by card or bank transfer
  • Payment plan by instalments
  • Employer invoice with a purchase order

Our commitments

Plans change. We've made that easy.

Resit guarantee

If you don't pass assessment, you can resit once with the next intake at no extra cost.

Substitution

Employers can substitute another eligible participant in the early weeks of the program at no cost. Contact us for more information.

Transfer

Move to the next intake once at no cost if work or life gets in the way.

Refunds

Set out in our enrolment terms. Contact us for more information.

Payment questions

Can my employer pay part of the cost?

Yes. We can split an invoice between you and your employer. Our business case template helps you request funding.

Is the cost tax deductible?

Self-education expenses connected to your current work may be deductible in some circumstances. Check with your tax adviser or the ATO; we can't give tax advice.
