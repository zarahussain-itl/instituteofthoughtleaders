About ITL

An institution built for workplaces that are evolving.

The Institute of Thought Leaders certifies corporate professionals to coach in workplace wellbeing, using evidence-based methods and assessment on real coaching practice. We believe wellbeing and performance aren't competing goals. When people are supported to work at their best, organisations win and so do their people.

Our vision

To be Australia's most trusted name in workplace wellbeing and leadership development, known for results that can be measured, reported and proven.

Our mission

We bring clarity to how organisations and their people work, so businesses achieve peak performance and every employee can thrive at their full potential.

Our values

What we hold ourselves to.

Integrity

We tell clients and students the truth, including what the data shows and what it doesn't. Our methods, reporting and pricing are open to scrutiny.

Do no harm

The wellbeing of every person we work with comes first. We practise within our scope, refer on when needed and hold our practitioners to rigorous ethical standards.

Loyalty to the person

Even when an organisation is paying, our duty of care is to the individual in front of us. We protect confidentiality and act in each person's genuine interest.

Earned prosperity

Our growth is a result of real, tangible improvement in workplaces, never a substitute for it. We measure our success by our clients' success.

Conscious evolution

Workplaces must evolve with how people really think, feel and perform. We help shape cultures where healthy, human-centred ways of working become the norm.

How we work

Certification for professionals and the organisations they work in.

We certify professionals

Specialist coaching certifications for corporate insiders who want to work in corporate wellbeing.

We build capability for employers

Sponsored places and private cohorts that give organisations their own certified coaches.

We grow a practitioner network

Over time, ITL graduates form a credentialed network of corporate wellbeing coaches.
