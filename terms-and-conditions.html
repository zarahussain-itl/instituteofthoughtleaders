Policies

Enrolment terms and conditions

Last updated October 2026

These terms apply when you, or your employer on your behalf, enrol in an ITL certification. "We" means Zara Hussain, trading as the Institute of Thought Leaders (ABN 49 122 630 940). "You" means the participant and, for sponsored places, the sponsoring organisation.

1. Eligibility

Participants need meaningful corporate experience, typically five years or more, and must meet any extra entry requirements listed for their certification. We may ask for evidence and may decline an enrolment that does not meet them, with a full refund.

2. Enrolment

Your place is confirmed when we accept your enrolment in writing and receive payment, or a purchase order for employer invoices. Places in each intake are limited and confirmed in order.

3. Fees and payment

  • Fees are confirmed in writing in your enrolment offer and are in AUD, including GST.
  • Payment plans are available, with the first instalment due on enrolment. Contact us for plan details.
  • Employer invoices are due by the date shown on the invoice.
  • If a payment is overdue, we may pause access to the program until it is paid.

4. Cancellations and refunds

  • Before the start date: refund or transfer terms are set out in your enrolment offer. Contact us for more information.
  • After the program starts: no refund, but you may transfer or substitute as set out below.

Request a cancellation in writing to info@instituteofthoughtleaders.com.

5. Transfers

You may transfer once to the next available intake of the same certification at no cost. Contact us about further transfers. Work you have completed and passed carries over.

6. Substitution for employer-sponsored places

A sponsoring organisation may substitute another eligible participant at no cost in the early weeks of the program. The new participant joins at the current point of the program and will be offered catch-up support.

7. Attendance and completion

To certify, you must attend the face-to-face intensive, attend or watch the recordings of live sessions, complete the required practicum hours and pass assessment within the time set out in your enrolment offer.

8. Assessment, resits and appeals

Assessment follows the ITL Coaching Competency Standard. If you don't pass, you may resit once with the next intake at no extra cost. Contact us about further resits. You can appeal an assessment decision through our complaints and appeals process.

9. Recordings and practice clients

You must get written consent from anyone you coach before recording a session, using our consent form, and handle recordings as set out in our privacy policy.

10. Course materials

Course materials belong to ITL. You may use them for your own learning and coaching practice, but not copy, share or resell them, or use them to run training for others.

11. Conduct

You agree to follow the ITL Code of Ethics and to treat faculty and other participants with respect. We may suspend or end an enrolment for a serious breach, after giving you a chance to respond.

12. Changes by ITL

We may change dates, venues or faculty when needed, and will tell you as early as we can. If we cancel an intake, you can choose a full refund or a place in another intake.

13. Your consumer rights

Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or modifies any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law.

14. Liability

ITL certifications are professional training. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for how graduates use what they learn in their own coaching practice.

15. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.
