Corporate Performance Coach

A practical corporate coaching certification grounded in cognitive-behavioural coaching and the neuroscience of attention, motivation and habit. Coach people to sharper performance, communication and decision-making.

You'll learn to

  • Coach towards clear, measurable goals using solution-focused methods
  • Apply CBT-informed techniques to shift unhelpful thinking at work
  • Use neuroscience on stress and habit to build lasting behaviour change
  • Recognise when someone needs to be referred to other support

Options for employers

Who it's for

Corporate insiders, not classroom theorists.

ITL students are empathetic people who understand the pressure, politics and pace of real organisations. That experience is what makes our graduates trusted inside corporates.

HR and People & Culture professionals

You see burnout and attrition every day and want the tools to deliver wellbeing, not just administer it.

Experienced leaders seeking purpose

Developing people is the part of your job you love most. Make it your profession.

Corporate career changers

After a restructure or a turning point, return to corporates as a trusted adviser.

Practitioners with corporate backgrounds

Coaches, counsellors and therapists who want specialist credentials for organisational work.

Entry requirements

  • Meaningful corporate experience, typically five years or more
  • Genuine empathy and a desire to improve working lives
  • Commitment to ethical practice and ITL's do-no-harm standard

ITL may not suit you if

  • You want a general life coaching qualification
  • You aren't interested in organisational contexts
  • You're looking for a quick, low-effort certificate

Common questions

Before you enrol

Do I need coaching experience?

No. We teach the coaching and practitioner skills. What you need is solid corporate experience.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Programs are structured for professional development sponsorship, with clear learning outcomes and minimal time away from work.

Is this a clinical qualification?

No. ITL certifications prepare you for workplace wellbeing and coaching work. They don't qualify you to diagnose or treat mental health conditions.
